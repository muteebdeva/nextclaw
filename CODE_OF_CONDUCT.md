# Code of Conduct

NextClaw is a small open-source project and we want it to stay welcoming.

- Be kind and assume good intent. Critique ideas, not people.
- No harassment, hate speech, or personal attacks — in issues, PRs, or discussions.
- Respect maintainers' time: search existing issues before opening a new one,
  and keep reports reproducible.

Unacceptable behavior may result in a warning or a ban from the project
spaces, at the maintainers' discretion. Reports can be made through any
channel listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
